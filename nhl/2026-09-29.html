Anytime Goal Sheet

Chance of scoring at least one goal (regulation or overtime; shootouts don't count), from expected ice time at even strength and on the power play, each player's shot quality (expected goals per 60), finishing skill (heavily regressed), the opponent's defense, the opposing starting goalie, power-play opportunities, home ice and back-to-backs. No betting lines are used. Fair odds are no-vig. Lineups assume the 12 forwards and 6 defensemen with the most expected ice time; check the morning skate for scratches. Backtest (2024–25, 2025–26): calibrated within about 1 point from 5% to 35%, slightly high above 35%.
