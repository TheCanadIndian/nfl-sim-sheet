Goal Sheet Results

Each night's list is graded against who actually scored (regulation and overtime; shootouts don't count). Only skaters who played are counted. A well-calibrated list has actual scorers close to expected, and players rated around 30% scoring about 30% of the time. Backtest reference (2024–25 and 2025–26): log-loss 0.387–0.391, top 3 per game scored about 35%.
