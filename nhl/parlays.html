NHL parlays

Every parlay the NHL page showed, frozen at the last price check before each day's first game (priced at Novig, Kalshi and Hard Rock), then graded from the box scores. A parlay loses on any losing leg; legs on players who didn't play, and pushes, drop out as they do at the books. Expected = our chance when shown. Return = flat 1-unit stakes at the prices shown (cards without book prices count at their fair payout).
