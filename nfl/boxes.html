NFL Box Score Accuracy highly experimental

Each game’s projected box score is frozen at tip-off / kickoff exactly as the page showed it (With Vegas) and graded against the real box score after the game. Error = how far the box’s number was from what happened, on average per player. A player the box listed who didn’t play counts as 0.
