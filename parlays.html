Parlays

Every parlay the site builds, in one place: each sport’s Today’s card (4 a day: 3 core + 1 longshot, overs only, every leg at your books and from a different game), the multisport all-day longshots, and every card from the sport pages. Frozen at each parlay’s first game and graded on the Profit page. Same-game parlays stay on each game’s page (books don’t pay them as the legs multiplied). Updated about every 15 minutes before games.
