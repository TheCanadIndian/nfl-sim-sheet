How to read the numbers

Median
The middle outcome. Half the simulated games land above it, half below. "Median 64 receiving yards" means over and under 63.5 are about a coin flip.
The bar (8 in 10 games)
The thin line spans where 8 of every 10 simulated games landed; the darker band is the middle half. A short bar means a steady player, a long one means a boom-or-bust week.
Chance to score
How often the player scored in the simulations. 40% means about 2 games in 5.
Fair odds
What that chance is worth with no bookmaker margin. If a book offers a better price than the fair odds (for example +150 when fair is +120), the model thinks that side is good value. If the book's price is shorter, it isn't.
Price a line
On the NFL page, open Price a line, pick a player and stat, and type the book's line to get fair over/under odds.
Matchups (▲ ▼)
Arrows flag unusually easy or tough matchups. They explain a projection; the projection already includes them, so don't add them on top.
Results
Every past week is graded against the real box scores, so you can see how often the ranges held and which players the model missed.

Projections come from public data (nflverse play-by-play, the NHL's own API, Open-Meteo weather). NFL games are anchored to the Vegas spread and total; the market-blind and NHL versions use no betting lines. They're estimates, not guarantees.
