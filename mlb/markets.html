How to read this

Games, then players
Each game opens to its game lines (winner, total, spread) and every player with a market. Tap a player to see all their lines. The chip on each player is their best edge.
Edge
Our chance minus the price you'd pay, after Kalshi's fee, on whichever side we like. Green = we lean that side; brighter = bigger. "Market ahead" = the market has been more accurate than us on that type so far.
Money on each side
The bar splits the dollars you could spend within 3¢ of the best price: blue = YES / over, gray = NO / under. Under $100 is thin; $1,000+ is a real market.
Thin vs tight
Thin (2¢+ gap between buy and sell) markets get less attention; so far our NHL scorer chances have beaten the price there, while the market has been sharper in tight (1¢) ones.
