Profit

Everything the site recommends, graded at the price it showed, 1 unit per bet. Prediction-market picks follow the site's rules on the last pregame quote (Kalshi includes its fee); Value tags use the tagged book's price when the tag first appeared, and Beat close compares it with that book's last price before the game; for prediction-market picks Beat close is Pinnacle's fair (no-vig) closing chance minus the price paid, fee included, when Pinnacle priced the same bet (positive = better than the sharpest closing line, the quickest sign of a real edge); parlays are every card on the Plays / Parlays tabs, frozen before their first game. Verdicts: Proven = the 95% range of the return is above zero (30+ bets); Losing = below zero; Promising = beats the closing line by a clear margin; Too early = fewer than 30 bets.
