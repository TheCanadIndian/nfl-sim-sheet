Opening lines

Every over price is recorded from the first time a book posts it (the site checks about every 15 minutes, and checks games it has no prices for every hour until a book posts). Once the game starts, each opener is scored against the closing sharp fair at the same line: the fair × the opening payout − 1 (positive = the opening price beat the close). “Late” is the same book’s last pregame price, scored the same way. Early-value openers (already 3%+ under the sharp fair when posted) are also graded as bets on the Profit page.
