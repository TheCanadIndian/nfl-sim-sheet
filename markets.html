Markets

What prediction markets (Kalshi, Polymarket) charge for an outcome, next to our chance of it, and how much money is really there. Longshots (first and anytime scorers, ladder tails) come first.

How to read this

Ours vs price
The bar shows our chance (blue tick) against what the market charges (gray fill). If our tick is past the fill, we think YES is cheap.
Edge
Our chance minus the price you'd pay, after Kalshi's fee. Green = we lean that side; brighter = bigger. Shown for whichever side (YES or NO) we like.
Money at this price
Dollars you could spend within 3 cents of the best price. Under $100 is thin; $1,000+ is a real market. "Exit" is what you could sell back before the game.
Thin vs tight
The spread is the gap between the best buy and sell price. Tight (1¢) markets are actively traded and have usually priced in what we know. Thin (2¢+) markets get less attention; so far our NHL scorer chances have beaten the price there, while the market has been sharper in tight ones.
Big edges in busy markets
When a deep, actively traded market disagrees with us by a lot, it usually knows something (injury, role change). Check the news first.
